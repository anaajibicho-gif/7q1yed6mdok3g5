Bars show how each post's new votes split. Lines show each candidate's running share of all votes counted. What the county's ballot-level file shows

From the county's cast vote record, exported November 4 at 2:26 AM. It covers only ballots counted by then, so it won't change as new results post. Its final ranked-choice totals match the county's in all three races. This page shows combined totals only, with no ballot-level or precinct-level data. Uncounted mail isn't included, so these patterns are clues rather than results, and scan date isn't the same as the date a voter returned a ballot.
