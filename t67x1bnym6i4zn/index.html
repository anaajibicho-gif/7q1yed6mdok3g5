OUSD late-vote what-if

Set how much worse the leader does in the uncounted votes, separately where they're ahead and where they're behind, and see where each race ends. Precinct counts are final-round votes from the county's cast vote record as of Post Election Update #1 (Nov 6, 5:05 PM); they add up exactly to the county's totals. Votes left are the same estimate as the main dashboard: the county's uncounted-ballot report (Nov 6, 6 PM) for the whole county, split by each district's share of returned mail, turned into votes at this year's rate from the ballot file, minus the votes already counted.
